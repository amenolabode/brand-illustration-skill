# Cowrywise analytical example

This example demonstrates a complete translation. Do not reuse it as a default aesthetic.

## Strategic chain

- Tenet: make money make sense; make wealth-building clear and approachable.
- Source world: guilloché and security-print engraving used on banknotes and valuable documents.
- Source meaning: money, authenticity, security, precision and controlled complexity.
- Source behavior: repeated curves, rosettes, interlocking meshes, parallel hatching and precise registration.
- Translation: simplify engraving into colourful meshes; clip it into familiar symbols; construct objects as stacked or folded paper money.

The design studio’s published rationale explicitly identifies colourful guilloché-inspired patterns and a 3D language representing looking at things from all sides: https://dadesign.studio/work/cowrywise

## Shared grammar

- Symbols: safe, target, badge, calendar, bulb, heart, shield, chart and naira.
- Surface: guilloché meshes and parallel hatching clipped to pale faces.
- Structure: orthographic/isometric construction with consistent lower-right extrusion.
- Material: printed, laminated, folded or stacked paper rather than plastic.
- Palette: electric-blue field, deep ink-blue depth, pale multicolour faces.
- Motion: one explanatory action at a time—line draws, arrow sweeps, date changes, badge settles.

## Three modes

### Technical scene

Isometric grid and exploded interface; wireframe, neutral volume and branded material states.

### Product pictograms

Flat readable symbols whose secondary surfaces carry the engraving grammar; motion explains product behavior.

### Editorial objects

Centred objects on a saturated field; pale patterned front, deep sidewall and striped stacked-paper edge.

## Execution qualities to keep when the source changes

- Guilloché is about 1px linework at low contrast (pale blue on powder blue). It never competes with the silhouette.
- Three rendering modes: isometric wireframe→volume→material scene, flat filled icons, and oblique-extruded objects.
- Electric blue is the only saturated colour. Navy is depth only. Pastels are faces only.
- Everything is flat vector, with no light source, no grain and no cast shadow.
- Motion explains state, one action at a time (target fills, date ticks, scene builds up), rather than decorating.

## Why it works

Guilloché is not only texture. It connects the product category to trust and authenticity, while paper construction, seals, rosettes, stacked edges and diagrammatic depth extend the same source world. The direction would retain coherence even if fine mesh detail were reduced at small sizes.
